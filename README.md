Data Collection & Storage: This extension stores user preferences, such as custom background settings and enabled feature toggles, locally on your device using the chrome.storage API.

Purpose: This information is stored exclusively to maintain your custom configuration and ensure the extension functions as intended.

Data Sharing: No data is transmitted to external servers, sold to third parties, or used for tracking purposes.

User Control: You can clear your settings at any time by uninstalling the extension or resetting your preferences via the extension settings menu.
