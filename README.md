Data Collection & Storage: This extension stores user preferences, such as custom background settings and enabled feature toggles, locally on your device using the chrome.storage API. This extension is not able to obtain any of your personal information.

Purpose: This information is stored exclusively to maintain your custom configuration such as background themes and whatnot to ensure the extension functions as intended.

Data Sharing: No data is transmitted to external servers, sold to third parties, or used for tracking purposes.

User Control: You can clear your settings at any time by uninstalling the extension or resetting your preferences via the extension settings menu.
